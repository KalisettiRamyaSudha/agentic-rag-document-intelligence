SECTION 2: INSURING AGREEMENT - DWELLING COVERAGE (COVERAGE A)
We will pay for direct physical loss to the property described in the Declarations caused by a peril insured against. This includes the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling. Materials and supplies located on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter, or repair the dwelling or other structures on the residence premises are also covered under this section. If a covered loss occurs, we will pay the cost to repair or replace the damaged property, subject to the policy limits and applicable deductible. Coverage is provided on an open-peril basis, meaning all risks of direct physical loss are covered unless specifically excluded.

SECTION 3: OTHER STRUCTURES COVERAGE (COVERAGE B)
We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space, including structures connected to the dwelling by only a fence, utility line, or similar connection. This includes detached garages, storage sheds, fences, swimming pools, and guest houses. Other structures used in whole or in part for business purposes or held for rental to any person not a tenant of the dwelling are not covered, unless used solely as a private garage. The limit for Coverage B is 10% of the Coverage A limit unless otherwise endorsed.

SECTION 4: PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE (COVERAGE C)
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. At your request, we will cover personal property owned by others while the property is on the residence premises. Personal property usually situated at an insured's residence other than the residence premises is subject to a limit of 10% of the Coverage C limit or $1,000, whichever is greater. Special limits of liability apply to certain categories of personal property. These include: cash, bank notes, and coins ($200 limit); securities, accounts, deeds, and manuscripts ($1,500 limit); watercraft including trailers and equipment ($1,500 limit); jewelry, watches, furs, and precious stones ($1,500 limit for loss by theft); firearms and related equipment ($2,500 limit); silverware and goldware ($2,500 limit); business property on the residence premises ($2,500 limit); business property away from the residence premises ($500 limit); electronic data processing equipment ($5,000 limit).

SECTION 5: LOSS OF USE COVERAGE (COVERAGE D)
If a covered peril makes the residence premises unfit to live in, we will pay the additional living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. Payment shall be for the shortest time required to repair or replace the premises, or for your household to permanently relocate. If a civil authority prohibits you from use of the residence premises as a result of direct damage to neighboring premises by a peril insured against, we cover the resulting additional living expenses and fair rental value for a period not exceeding two weeks. The Coverage D limit is 30% of the Coverage A amount.

SECTION 6: PERSONAL LIABILITY COVERAGE (COVERAGE E)
If a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies, we will pay up to the Coverage E limit of liability for the damages for which the insured is legally liable. We will provide a defense at our expense by counsel of our choice, even if the suit is groundless or fraudulent. Our duty to defend ends when the amount we pay for damages equals our limit of liability. This coverage does not apply to bodily injury or property damage which is expected or intended by the insured, arising out of business activities, arising out of the rendering of professional services, or arising out of premises owned by the insured but not an insured location.

SECTION 7: MEDICAL PAYMENTS COVERAGE (COVERAGE F)
We will pay the necessary medical expenses incurred within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury. Medical expenses include surgical, X-ray, dental, ambulance, hospital, professional nursing, prosthetic devices, and funeral services. This coverage applies to a person injured on the insured location with the permission of any insured, or away from the insured location if the bodily injury arises out of a condition on the insured location, is caused by the activities of an insured, or is caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured. This coverage does not apply to any insured, to any person eligible to receive benefits under workers' compensation, or to any person injured on the insured location due to nuclear hazard.

SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS - PROPERTY COVERAGE
The following perils are excluded from property coverage under this policy:
8.1 FLOOD: We do not cover loss caused by flood, including surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of any body of water, whether or not driven by wind. Mudflow and mudslide are also excluded. Flood coverage may be available through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP).
8.2 EARTHQUAKE: We do not cover loss caused by earthquake, including land shock waves or tremors before, during, or after a volcanic eruption. Earthquake coverage may be added by endorsement for an additional premium.
8.3 ORDINANCE OR LAW: We do not cover loss caused by enforcement of any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair, or demolition of a building or other structure, unless the Ordinance or Law Coverage endorsement is attached.
8.4 POWER FAILURE: We do not cover loss caused by power failure if the failure occurs away from the residence premises.
8.5 NEGLECT: We do not cover loss caused by neglect of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of loss.
8.6 WAR: We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, or revolution.
8.7 NUCLEAR HAZARD: We do not cover loss caused by nuclear hazard, including nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination.
8.8 INTENTIONAL LOSS: We do not cover loss arising out of any act an insured commits or conspires to commit with the intent to cause a loss.
8.9 GOVERNMENTAL ACTION: We do not cover loss caused by seizure or destruction of property by order of governmental authority, unless the action is taken to prevent the spread of fire.
8.10 MOLD AND FUNGUS: We do not cover loss caused by the presence, growth, proliferation, spread or any activity of mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or other microorganism. Limited mold coverage of $10,000 may be available by endorsement.

SECTION 9: EXCLUSIONS - LIABILITY COVERAGE
The following are excluded from liability coverage under this policy:
9.1 MOTOR VEHICLE LIABILITY: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, loading, or unloading of motor vehicles owned or operated by any insured.
9.2 WATERCRAFT LIABILITY: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of watercraft exceeding 26 feet in overall length.
9.3 AIRCRAFT LIABILITY: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of any aircraft.
9.4 BUSINESS LIABILITY: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of or in connection with a business conducted from the insured location or engaged in by any insured.
9.5 PROFESSIONAL LIABILITY: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering of or failure to render professional services.
9.6 COMMUNICABLE DISEASE: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured.
9.7 CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE: We do not cover bodily injury or property damage arising out of the use, sale, manufacture, delivery, transfer, or possession of a controlled substance.

SECTION 10: CONDITIONS
10.1 INSURABLE INTEREST AND LIMIT OF LIABILITY: Even if more than one person has an insurable interest in the property covered, we will not be liable in any one loss for more than the applicable limit of liability.
10.2 DUTIES AFTER LOSS: In case of loss to covered property, the insured must give prompt notice to us or our agent, protect the property from further damage, cooperate with us in the investigation of a claim, prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing quantity, description, and amount of loss, and submit to us within 60 days after the loss a signed sworn proof of loss.
10.3 LOSS SETTLEMENT: Covered property losses are settled at actual cash value at the time of loss but not more than the amount required to repair or replace. If the cost to repair or replace is more than $5,000 and more than 5% of the Coverage A limit, we will pay replacement cost only if the property is actually repaired or replaced within 180 days of the loss.
10.4 MORTGAGE CLAUSE: If a mortgagee is named in this policy, any loss payable under Coverage A or B shall be paid to the mortgagee and the insured as interests appear. The mortgagee's right of recovery shall not be defeated by any act or neglect of the insured.
10.5 SUIT AGAINST US: No action can be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all policy terms. Any action must be started within two years after the date of loss.
10.6 CANCELLATION: You may cancel this policy at any time by returning it to us or by notifying us in writing. We may cancel this policy by mailing written notice to the insured at the address shown in the Declarations at least 30 days before the effective date of cancellation, or 10 days if cancellation is for nonpayment of premium.
10.7 SUBROGATION: An insured may waive in writing before a loss all rights of recovery against any person. If a claim is paid, an insured must transfer to us all rights of recovery against any person and must cooperate with us in enforcing these rights.

SECTION 14: CLAIMS PROCEDURE AND REPORTING
14.1 REPORTING A CLAIM: To report a claim, contact our claims department at 1-800-555-CLAIM or through our online portal at www.insuranceco.com/claims. Claims should be reported within 72 hours of the loss event. Late reporting may result in delays or limitations on coverage.
14.2 FIRST NOTICE OF LOSS (FNOL): When reporting a claim, you will need to provide: your policy number, date and time of loss, description of what happened, description of damage or injury, police or fire department report number if applicable, names and contact information of any witnesses, and photographs of the damage if possible.
14.3 INVESTIGATION: We have the right to investigate any claim. You must cooperate with our investigation, including providing recorded statements, submitting to examination under oath, and producing financial records if the claim involves a loss of earnings.
14.4 ADJUSTER ASSIGNMENT: An adjuster will be assigned to your claim within 48 hours of the FNOL. The adjuster will contact you to schedule an inspection of the damaged property. For catastrophe events, adjuster assignment may take longer due to volume.
14.5 PROOF OF LOSS: A signed and sworn proof of loss must be submitted within 60 days of our request. The proof of loss must state the time and cause of loss, interest of the insured and all others in the property, actual cash value and replacement cost of each item, all encumbrances on the property, other insurance covering the loss, and changes in title or occupancy during the policy term.

We will have the right and duty to defend the insured against any suit seeking those damages. SECTION 21: GENERAL LIABILITY - EXCLUSIONS

21.3 POLLUTION: This insurance does not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of the actual, alleged, or threatened discharge, dispersal, seepage, migration, release, or escape of pollutants at any location.
21.4 PROFESSIONAL LIABILITY: This insurance does not apply to bodily injury or property damage arising out of the rendering of or failure to render any professional service, including but not limited to legal, accounting, advertising, architecture, engineering, and financial services.
21.5 DAMAGE TO PROPERTY: This insurance does not apply to property damage to property you own, rent, or occupy; property loaned to you; personal property in the care, custody, or control of the insured; or that particular part of real property on which you or any contractor working on your behalf is performing operations.
21.6 RECALL OF PRODUCTS: This insurance does not apply to damages claimed for any loss, cost, or expense incurred by you or others for the loss of use, withdrawal, recall, inspection, repair, replacement, adjustment, removal, or disposal of your product if such product is withdrawn or recalled from the market.
21.7 CYBER LIABILITY: This insurance does not apply to bodily injury, property damage, or personal injury arising out of the loss, misuse, or unauthorized access to electronic data, including but not limited to data breaches, ransomware attacks, and unauthorized system access. Cyber liability coverage may be obtained through a separate cyber insurance policy.
